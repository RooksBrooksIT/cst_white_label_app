## 1. INTRODUCTION & OVERVIEW

eBrick ("Platform", "App", "eBrick Application") is an enterprise white-label construction management software solution developed and operated by Rooks Technologies. The platform is engineered to assist construction companies, builders, developers, contractors, and project organizations ("Organisation") in organizing, tracking, and managing construction workflows, site expenses, material inventory, workforce attendance, petty cash, architectural documentation, and project reporting.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected, stored, and processed through our mobile applications, web portals, APIs, and associated services.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data, site operations data, and technical telemetry are collected, processed, disclosed, retained, and protected in compliance with applicable data protection legislation, including:
- The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (India);
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA - India);
- Google Play Developer Policy and Apple App Store Review Guidelines regarding User Data, Device Permissions, and Account Deletion;
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other international standards where applicable.

By accessing, registering with, or using the eBrick App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. SCOPE & USER ROLES (DATA CONTROLLER VS. DATA PROCESSOR)

### 2.1 Multi-Tenant White-Label Architecture
eBrick is delivered as a white-label multi-tenant platform. In this structure:
- **The Organisation (Customer Organization / Builder / Main Contractor)** is the **Data Fiduciary / Data Controller** with respect to the project data, site records, and user accounts it creates, authorizes, or assigns.
- **Rooks Technologies (eBrick)** acts primarily as the **Data Processor** that hosts, maintains, secures, and provides the cloud software infrastructure.
- Rooks Technologies acts as a **Data Controller** solely with regard to direct billing, subscription account registration of the primary Organisation representative, and direct customer support inquiries.

### 2.2 User Personas Covered
This policy applies to all individuals accessing the platform across all role levels:
1. **Organisation Admins**: Primary account holders, company directors, and executive system administrators.
2. **Project Managers & Engineers**: Users overseeing project phases, approving budget requests, assigning sites, and tracking schedules.
3. **Site Supervisors**: Field personnel responsible for logging daily site entries, material receipts, workforce attendance, petty cash claims, and photographic progress.
4. **Contractors & Subcontractors**: Specialized trade partners submitting bills, worker logs, and task completions.
5. **Workers & Labourers**: Personnel whose names, trade categories, presence, and daily wage records are maintained by the Organisation/Supervisor.
6. **Clients / Property Owners (Customers)**: End clients viewing project progress, site updates, and architectural drawings.

## 3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect information necessary to deliver construction management operations, maintain authentication, verify site presence, and secure the platform.

### 3.1 Information Provided Directly by the Organisation
- **Company Profile**: Legal business name, company registration/GST number, registered office address, primary business phone, official email, and corporate logo/branding assets.
- **Billing & Subscription Records**: Subscription plan selections, invoice details, transaction identifiers, and contact details of billing managers (note: sensitive credit/debit card numbers are handled directly by certified payment gateways and are never stored on eBrick servers).

### 3.2 User Accounts & Profile Data (Created by Organisation or User)
- Full name, mobile phone number, email address, password (stored in salted/hashed cryptographic formats).
- Assigned system role (e.g., Supervisor, Manager, Organization Admin, Client).
- Designated construction site(s) and project assignment mappings.
- Identification details or emergency contact numbers (where configured by the Organisation).

### 3.3 Construction Site Operations Data
To fulfill the operational purpose of the application, users input and upload operational data:
- **Daily Site Entries**: Daily logs of construction activities, work summaries, weather notes, and task progress.
- **Material & Inventory Tracking**: Material receipts, consumption quantities, supplier bills, delivery notes, and tool movement logs.
- **Petty Cash & Site Expenses**: Claims, advance receipts, vendor invoices, fuel, transportation, and food expenditure records.
- **Workforce Attendance & Wage Tracking**: Worker counts, worker names, trade categories (masons, carpenters, helpers), daily attendance punch records, overtime logs, and payout calculations.
- **Architectural Drawings & Documents**: Blueprints, CAD designs, site plans, structural documentation, PDFs, and inspection checklists.

### 3.4 Media, Images & Device Files
- **Progress Photographs**: Images captured on-site documenting structural progress, material quality, safety violations, or completed milestones.
- **Bill & Receipt Scans**: Photos of cash vouchers, supplier bills, weighbridge slips, and challans uploaded for audit verification.

### 3.5 Device Permissions & Hardware Data
The eBrick mobile application requests specific device permissions to enable field features:

| Permission | Technical Name | Purpose in eBrick | Optional / Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Location (Precise & Coarse)** | `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` | Verifies that supervisor/worker attendance check-ins and daily site entries occur at the designated geofenced construction site. Prevents fraudulent off-site entries. | Required for site check-in & geofenced entries |
| **Camera** | `CAMERA` | Allows supervisors and managers to photograph daily site construction progress, material deliveries, damaged tools, and physical expense receipts directly within the app. | Required for live photo verification |
| **Storage / Media / Photos** | `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` | Enables uploading existing blueprints, construction contracts, inspection PDFs, and receipt images from the device gallery or files. | Required for file attachments |
| **Push Notifications** | `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Sends critical project alerts, task assignments, petty cash approval notifications, and material stock replenishment alerts. | Optional (user can toggle in device settings) |
| **Internet & Network State** | `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Synchronizes offline site logs with cloud servers and detects active network connectivity. | Required for cloud synchronization |

### 3.6 Automated Technical, Log & Telemetry Information
- **Device Identifiers**: Hardware model, device brand, operating system and version, unique device tokens, screen resolution, and language settings.
- **Network & Connectivity**: IP address, internet service provider, connection type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G), and signal state.
- **App Telemetry & Crash Logs**: Crash traces, API latency logs, error stack traces, and interaction timestamps utilized to diagnose platform stability and prevent crashes.

## 4. PURPOSE & USE OF COLLECTED DATA

eBrick processes data strictly for legitimate operational and business purposes:
1. **Core Platform Delivery**: Authenticating user credentials, segregating tenant company data, and displaying role-specific dashboards.
2. **Construction Operational Auditing**: Enabling real-time project oversight, tracking material consumption against phase estimates, and reconciling petty cash vouchers against physical bills.
3. **Geographical Verification**: Verifying that field supervisors, contractors, and labour entries originate at authorized physical site coordinates.
4. **Communication & Collaboration**: Relaying real-time push alerts, status changes on approval requests, and site notice broadcasts.
5. **Audit Trails & Security**: Maintaining non-repudiable logs of financial transactions, daily site entries, and role modifications to deter fraudulent accounting or unauthorized modifications.
6. **Platform Performance & Stability**: Analyzing crash reports, optimizing API responsiveness, and enhancing offline-first caching capabilities.
7. **Legal & Compliance Obligations**: Adhering to statutory tax, accounting, dispute resolution, and contractual mandates.

## 7. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

We implement comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal and construction records against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:
- **Encryption in Transit**: All communications between the mobile application, web portals, and backend servers are secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3) and HTTPS.
- **Encryption at Rest**: Databases, cloud file storage, and server backups are encrypted using standard AES-256 bit encryption.
- **Authentication & Authorization**: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensures strict compartmentalization between roles and prevents cross-tenant access between different organizations.
- **Password Protection**: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed; plaintext passwords are never accessible to platform operators.
- **Access Logging & Audits**: Privileged infrastructure access is restricted to authorized engineering personnel through Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and audit-logged jump boxes.

*Note: While we adhere to rigorous commercial security standards, no electronic storage system or wireless internet transmission is 100% infallible. Users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and mobile devices.*

## 8. DATA RETENTION & STORAGE DURATION

- **Active Accounts**: We retain personal and operational site data for the duration of the Organisation's active subscription contract to maintain historical project records, warranty documentation, and audit histories.
- **Terminated / Inactive Accounts**: Upon expiration or cancellation of an Organisation's subscription, data is retained for an archival period (typically 90 days) to allow account reactivation or data export, following which it is queued for secure deletion or permanent anonymization, unless legal, tax, or regulatory standards dictate longer preservation.
- **Device Logs & Telemetry**: Technical diagnostic logs are automatically purged on a rolling 30 to 90-day cycle.

## 9. USER RIGHTS, DATA ACCESS & ACCOUNT / DATA DELETION

In compliance with Google Play Store policies, Apple App Store guidelines, and data protection laws (including DPDPA / GDPR):

### 9.1 Your Rights
Users have the right to:
1. **Access**: Request confirmation of whether their personal data is processed and obtain a copy.
2. **Correction / Rectification**: Request updates or corrections to incomplete or inaccurate personal records.
3. **Withdraw Consent**: Revoke location, camera, or notification permissions at any time via device OS settings.
4. **Data Deletion / Erasure**: Request complete deletion of their user profile and associated personal data.

### 9.2 Account and Data Deletion Process
- **Individual App Users (Supervisors, Managers, Contractors, Clients)**: As your account is provisioned by your employer or contracting Organisation, you may request account deactivation directly through your Organisation Administrator, or submit a deletion request to **support@rookstechnologies.com**.
- **Organisation Administrators**: The primary Organisation account owner may request complete organization decommissioning and irreversible deletion of all project databases, user profiles, and storage buckets by emailing **support@rookstechnologies.com** from the registered primary admin email address.
- **Processing Timeline**: Legitimate account deletion requests are verified and processed within **30 business days**, following which associated personal identifiers are permanently scrubbed from our production servers, except where retention is mandatory under statutory taxation or legal dispute statutes.
