3. PAYMENT GATEWAY & UPGRADE LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Payment Gateway: PayU Checkout Integration (`PayUCheckoutScreen`)
• Transaction Handling:
  - Generates secure transaction ID (`txnid`) via `PayUService`
  - Back-end verification via Firebase Cloud Functions
• Plan Upgrade Options:
  1. Immediate Upgrade: Activates new plan instantly and updates Firestore.
  2. Queued Upgrade: Schedules plan change to auto-activate when current 
     subscription period ends (`queuedPlan`, `queuedStartDate`, `queuedEndDate`).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Recurring Billing: Monthly, 6 Months, or Yearly recurring cycles.
• Support Contact: support@rookstechnologies.com
• Refund Request Window: Eligible within 7 days of initial purchase or renewal.
• Refund Processing Time: 5 to 7 business days to original payment method.
• Partial Usage: No refunds provided for partial subscription periods or 
  unused plan quotas.
